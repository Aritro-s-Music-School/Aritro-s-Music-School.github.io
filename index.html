About

With over 23 years of percussion experience and a deep foundation in jazz performance, I offer personalized instruction in drums and foundational piano. Since beginning my percussion journey in fourth grade (2001), I've explored concert percussion, marching percussion, jazz percussion and drum set, steel pan, vibraphone, Latin percussion, and auxiliary percussion.

Since 2017, I've been teaching piano with a focus on building strong foundational skills for beginners. My jazz piano expertise, developed since 2018, informs my teaching approach across all styles. While jazz is my specialty, I emphasize that most music styles share the same fundamental principles.
